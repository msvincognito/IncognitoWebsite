## Code of Conduct

MSV Incognito is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for all members, participants, and guests. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for behavior at all Incognito events and activities.

### Our Standards

**Expected Behavior:**
- Treat everyone with respect and consideration
- Communicate openly and thoughtfully
- Be inclusive and welcoming to newcomers
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Show empathy towards others

**Unacceptable Behavior:**
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Offensive comments related to personal characteristics
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Disruptive behavior at events
- Any form of bullying or threats

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the board immediately at [incognito@maastrichtuniversity.nl](mailto:incognito@maastrichtuniversity.nl) or speak to a board member in person.

All reports will be handled confidentially and with discretion.

### Enforcement

The MSV Incognito board reserves the right to take any action deemed appropriate in response to violations of this Code of Conduct, including:
- Verbal warning
- Removal from the event or activity
- Temporary or permanent suspension of membership
- Reporting to university authorities when applicable

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Incognito activities, including but not limited to:
- General assemblies and meetings
- Social events and parties
- Career days and workshops
- Online communication channels
- Any other Incognito-sponsored activity
