## Our Committees

Incognito is run by a dedicated group of committees, each responsible for a different aspect of student life. From parties to education, there's a committee for everyone.

### Active Committees

<div class="committee-grid">
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>aKtiE</h3>
    <p>Activity Committee — organizes social events, parties, and gatherings.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>hacKErs</h3>
    <p>IT Committee — maintains our digital infrastructure and organizes tech events.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>proKtiE</h3>
    <p>Promo Committee — handles marketing, design, and public relations.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>redaKtiE</h3>
    <p>Editorial Committee — produces the yearbook and writes newsletters.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>tasKE</h3>
    <p>Task Force — supports the board with various operational tasks.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>piKtiE</h3>
    <p>Picnic Committee — organizes casual outdoor social events.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>boeKtiE</h3>
    <p>Book Committee — manages the book sale and study materials.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="committee-card">
    <h3>fiKtiE</h3>

  </div>
