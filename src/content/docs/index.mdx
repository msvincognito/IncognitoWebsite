<div class="about-snippet">
  <h2>More Than a Study Association</h2>
  <div class="about-columns">
    <div class="about-col">
      <h3>Our Story</h3>
      <p>Founded on <strong>March 28, 1994</strong>, MSV Incognito began as a small group of computing enthusiasts at Maastricht University. Today, we are a thriving community that mirrors the rapid growth of the Department of Advanced Computing Sciences.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="about-col">
      <h3>Our Mission</h3>
      <p>We exist to enrich the <strong>social, professional, and educational</strong> lives of our members. Through tutoring, career days, social events, and industry partnerships, we bridge the gap between academia and the real world.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="about-col">
      <h3>Our Community</h3>
      <p>Incognito is run by students, for students. Our committees organize everything from study sessions and game nights to international trips and company visits. There is a place for everyone here.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
