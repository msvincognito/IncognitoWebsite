### About Our Yearbooks

Each year, the redaKtiE (editorial committee) produces a yearbook documenting the events, members, and memories of that academic year. Yearbooks are distributed to members at the end of the academic year.

### Want a Yearbook?

Yearbooks are typically included with membership. Contact the board for information about obtaining past editions.
