## Medtronic

![Medtronic Logo](/assets/sponsors/medtronic-logo.jpg)

Medtronic is a global leader in medical technology, services, and solutions. They develop therapies that improve the lives of millions of people around the world.

### About Medtronic

From pacemakers to surgical robots, Medtronic's innovations span a wide range of medical fields. Their work in data science and software engineering is critical to advancing healthcare technology.

### Career Opportunities

Medtronic offers internships and positions in software engineering, data analytics, and biomedical engineering.
