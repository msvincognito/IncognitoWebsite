## YER

![YER Logo](/assets/sponsors/yer-logo.svg)

YER is a specialized recruitment agency focused on technical and engineering professionals. They connect talented individuals with leading companies in the Netherlands.

### About YER

YER understands the technical sector and helps both starters and experienced professionals find their next career step. Their consultants have deep knowledge of the industries they serve.

### For Students

YER can help you find internships, graduation projects, and your first job after graduation.
