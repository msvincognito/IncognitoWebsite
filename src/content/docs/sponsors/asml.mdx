## ASML

![ASML Logo](/assets/sponsors/ASML_Holding_N.V._logo.svg)

ASML is a leading innovator in the global semiconductor industry. Working closely with our customers and partners, we provide lithography systems – hardware, software and services – that help chipmakers create more powerful, affordable and energy-efficient microchips. These chips power modern life and help address some of humanity's toughest challenges. We're a global team of over 44,000 people of 143 different nationalities and counting. Headquartered in Europe's top tech hub, the Brainport Eindhoven region in the Netherlands, our operations are spread across Europe, Asia and the US.

### Changing the world, one nanometer at a time

In the past four decades, we've grown from a small startup into a multinational company with over 60 locations worldwide and total net sales of €32.7 billion in 2025. Our customers include all the world's leading chipmakers, such as Intel and Samsung. Our holistic lithography solutions, software and services help them achieve their highest yields and best performance, driving the advancement of microchips.

### Cutting-edge innovation

Behind our innovations you'll find people who think ahead. Our researchers, engineers, scientists and manufacturing specialists, as well as our high-tech hardware and software experts, all work at the edge of what's technologically possible. In 2025 alone, we invested €4.7 billion in R&D, empowering our teams to drive innovation and to keep pushing the boundaries of technology. Collaboration is key at ASML – our professionals work in close-knit, multidisciplinary teams, sharing knowledge and ideas as they learn from one another.

### Own your career

At ASML, you'll find a place to grow, develop your skills and contribute to shaping the future. We have various tools in place to support you in building your career, from trainings to personal coaching, acceleration programs and more. Doing what we do requires unique perspectives and innovative ideas. That's why we're proud to have employees from diverse backgrounds. As part of our team, you'll have the flexibility and support to share your creative ideas and try new things. In our dynamic environment at the forefront of tech, we take your work-life balance and well-being seriously. We support you in taking the initiative to foster your health and happiness and make the most of your career with us.

If you want to join a dynamic, global collaboration committed to pushing technology to new limits, visit [www.asml.com/careers](https://www.asml.com/careers).
