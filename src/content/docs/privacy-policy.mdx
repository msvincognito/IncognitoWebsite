## Privacy Policy

MSV Incognito ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

### Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:
- **Personal Information:** Name, email address, student number, and contact details provided during membership registration
- **Event Participation:** Records of events you have attended or registered for
- **Communication:** Emails and messages sent to us
- **Website Usage:** Anonymous analytics data about how you use our website

### How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for:
- Managing your membership
- Communicating about events and activities
- Processing event registrations and payments
- Sending newsletters and updates (with your consent)
- Improving our services

### Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:
- University administration for membership verification
- Event partners when necessary for event organization
- Service providers who assist our operations

### Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing of your data
- Request data portability

### Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at [incognito@maastrichtuniversity.nl](mailto:incognito@maastrichtuniversity.nl).

### Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify members of significant changes.
